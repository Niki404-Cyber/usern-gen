Username Combination Generator

In the username pattern, * is replaced with a number (0-9) and # with a letter (a-z). You can enter a single pattern or multiple username/number patterns separated by , / / / |. Browse results page by page, copy any page of up to 50,000, or stream up to 50,000,000 (5 crore) usernames straight to a .txt file.
